# Carpe Diem : Study Concentration App 
 I have developed a php based web app which enables students to focus on their studies when studying from various multimedia devices such as Mobile, Laptop, Tablet etc. The feature of notification blocking helps avoid distractions by disabling notifications while studying. It also enables parents to monitor the academic progress of their wards by providing an exclusive parent informatics dashboard. Additionally, this tool will keep the parents abreast with the browsing history and internet use of their children. Above of all it comprises book solution, short revision notes, and has an AI based question generator to enable students develop the skill of complex problem-solving. Apart from this, it has an ai based web page and pdf summarizer that enables the students to understand the overall given chapter.
